### 0.1 ⛔ `risk.py` declared the entire recommendation IMPOSSIBLE — FIXED
`p_get_freshman()` tested `sy1 == 0` **alone**. That is precisely the error R2/R134 exist to
prevent: per-year quotas are optional, so an all-zero `sy1..sy6` means *no scheme in force*,
not *no freshman seats*. On 8/16 it began returning

```
UIC1561-01-00  p=0.0  IMPOSSIBLE
QRM1001-01-00  p=0.0  IMPOSSIBLE
UIC1551-01-00  p=0.0  IMPOSSIBLE
```

Nothing called it, so nothing broke. Fixed, and now verified to agree with
`eligibility.year_barred()` on all 179 sections.

> **The generalisable failure, and it is the important part of this document.**
> `risk.py` was deliberately written as a *shaped hole* for data that had not arrived —
> returning `1.0, 'NO DATA'` so the model was "numerically unchanged today." That design
> means **its first real execution happens when the data lands, and by then nobody is
> looking at it.** It was correct for as long as it was untested, and wrong from the
> instant it mattered. Every other "shaped hole" in this project has the same property.

> ## R111. Credit cap = 22, but the target is 18 BY CHOICE (Iden 2026-08-06)

R193 adds the reason 18 is actively good rather than merely chosen: tie-break rung ⓗ is
직전학기이수학점/수강가능학점, so **18/18 = 1.00 maxes it**, and that rung sets his mileage

**What is left of this finding is small but real:** `CR_HI = 21.0` sat in `research_v3.py`

### 1.2 ⛔ Seat competition — NOT closed, and now believed unmeasurable from this source
`MISSING_2026-08-10 §2.4` said the 8/14 data would supply obtainability, turning the
equal-swap lists into an ordered fallback chain. **It does not.** `atnlcPercpCnt` is not
section capacity (R248: constant across 분반 regardless of 강의실 — UIC2151 has 9 분반 in 4
rooms, all "3"). So `여석 = 정원 − 신청` is meaningless and no seat-competition number exists.

`fallback.py` gives the **cost** half of `cost × probability`. The probability half has no
data source we know of. This gap is not "waiting on a date" any more.

**⚠️ `test_v3.py`'s R248 leak guard is BROKEN on `difficulty.py`, and correctly so.**
`easy_tier_competition()` reads `atnlcPercpCnt` and returns it under the key
`seats_per_section`; `risk.p_win_bracket` prints it as `seats {…}`; and R190's argument is
phrased as "the top TWO of a **2-seat** section" throughout. Under R248 none of those labels
are justified. No *score* is affected — `easy_tier_competition` is called only from
`difficulty.py`'s `__main__`, and `p_hard_if_deferred` delegates to `risk.p_win_bracket`
which uses the mileage distribution, not the seat count. So this is contamination at the
level of **names and stated reasoning**, not arithmetic. Left failing on purpose: the
assertion is coarse, but what it is pointing at is real, and the fix is to re-derive R190's
"2-seat" premise rather than to relabel it.
