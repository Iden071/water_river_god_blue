## 4. THE OBJECTIVE

```
F(x) = r(x) + [ V(remainder after x) − V(reference) ]

r(x) = fast_score(tm, pm, fm)          weekly comfort, R210 three-mask
     + Σ YEAR_PEN(eff_year)            학년 gap, EARLY arm
     + CHAPEL_BONUS | CHAPEL_DEFER     +10.0 | −4.2
     + difficulty                      −D_LANG × Σ steps, plus P_hard × D_LANG if Lang deferred
```

### The three masks (R210)
| mask | scope |
|---|---|
| `tm` nominal hours | conflict detection only |
| `fm` fixed hours | all comfort scoring — 9am, lunch, runs, REST, Friday event |
| `pm` presence | the trip home |

`fm = pm | (tm & ~pm if '실시간' in mode else 0)`. Recorded-video hours are excluded from `fm`.
Mode counts: 대면강의 283 fully fixed · 블랜디드(동영상) 46 fixed / 9 shiftable ·
비대면(동영상) 2 shiftable · 비대면(실시간+동영상) 1 fixed.

⚠️ **A section with `fm = 0` costs the comfort score nothing.** Two sections in the catalogue
are in that position (`YCG1804-01`, `YCG1853-01`, both 비대면(동영상), 3 nominal hours each).
Under any objective where V does not discriminate, the search stacks them — see §6.

### fast_score decomposition
`fast_score = week_value(pm, fm) + Σ day_pen(day)`. Measured: `max day_pen[0]` over 200,000
random days = **exactly 0**. `week_value` monotonicity violations = **0 / 20,000**.
`fast_score(0,0,0) = 163.071` (the empty week).
`fast_score` itself is **not** monotone in occupancy — adding a course can fill a hole (R217).

## 6. ⛔ THE BLOCKING DEFECT

**The simulated V does not discriminate between futures.**

Measured on the 416-state table: **70 distinct values**. 27 states share exactly −50.419,
27 share −5.281, 26 share 61.439, 22 land exactly on V_REF (39.729). Within-branch spread
median 57.867.

Three causes, all in the ledger (`plan_model.py` `ITEMS`), not in the optimiser:

1. **`DM` (×12) and `FREE` (×5) carry no course codes.** 17 of ~40 remaining courses are
   interchangeable filler drawn lightest-first from one shared pool.
2. **All ~6 `ME` instances resolve to the single code `QRM2004`.** Two MEs in one semester pin
   the identical section, collide, and one is demoted to filler by R215.
3. **`QRM3003` and `MR5` have no observed sections in any term or campus** — permanent filler.

Ledger items with any resolvable section: **12 / 15**.

Consequence: V reduces to approximately "count of identifiable courses per semester", which is
the statistic R208 ruled insufficient. With V flat, the ranker maximises `r(x)` alone and
selects the `fm = 0` sections described in §4. The 32-branch V_SIM run produced
`#1 = defer LHP+SciRD+Lang, 4 FREE electives, week 96.670` on that mechanism.

**Fixing this means giving DM, FREE and the individual MEs real course identities.** It is not
an optimiser problem.

## 10. HAZARDS IN THE CODE

- **`rank2.py` cannot be reordered or reformatted above the literal line `    heap = []; cnt=[0]`** —
  `rank3.build()` execs its source text up to that string. Backup: `rank2.py.bak`.
- **`R2.LANG`** holds a 2-course literal in `rank2.py:27`. `rank4.py` and `rank4_branch.py`
  override it at runtime to `difficulty.LANG_ALL` (10 courses). Any consumer importing `rank2`
  without that override gets the narrow pool.
- **The elective → ledger item map is read from the section** (`rank4.item_of_section`, using
  `qcat` / `_qrm_me`). A hand-written code list previously overrode it and inverted #1 (R183).
- **The mount permits truncation but not deletion.** `rm` fails; `: > file` works. `reset`
  leaves zero-byte files, which `_read_json` treats as "not run".
- **Background processes do not survive between bash calls.** Long work must be chunked or
  made resumable.
- **`rank2.week_value`'s docstring opens "Iden lives at 국제 (dorm)".** That phrasing is a
  paraphrase, not Iden's words, and it is wrong — see Residence in §1. It sits **above**
  `rank2.py`'s exec marker and therefore cannot be edited. Read §1, not that docstring.
