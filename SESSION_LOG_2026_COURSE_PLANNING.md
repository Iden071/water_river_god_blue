## Part 8: MILEAGE COMPETITION DATA — LIVE CAPTURE (Aug 4, 2026)

### CRITICAL DISCOVERY: Max Mileage is a PER-COURSE CAP, not universal 36

The 36M cap in the guide is the *student-side* limit. Each course carries its own
`Max Mileage` ceiling set by the offering department. This changes bid strategy entirely.

| Course | Campus | Max Mileage cap | Min win | Max win | **Avg win** | 정원 | 참여 | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| UIC1251-01 World Lit: East Asian | 신촌 | **36** | 5 | 36 | **19.1** | 19 | 41 | 2.16:1 |
| ECO2102-01 미시경제학 (Micro) | 신촌 | **12** | 5 | 12 | **11.82** | 59 | 109 | 1.85:1 |

### ECO2102 (Micro) — full detail
- **Max Mileage = 12** (= 4 × 3 credits — the UIC First auto-deduct amount IS the ceiling)
- Avg winning bid **11.82 of 12** → essentially *everyone bids the max*; the course is
  decided almost entirely by tie-breaks, not by bid size.
- 학년별정원 (grade quotas): **1학년 = 0**, 2학년 = 35, 3학년 = 15, 4학년 = 9
  → **Freshmen cannot get ECO2102 at all.** Sophomore+ only. Confirms Micro/Macro
    cannot move earlier than Sem 3.
- 전공자 정원 45 (N) of 59 total seats → majors hold most seats; UIC econ-track students
  compete for the remainder.
- Tie-break rank 0 shows 학년 1 with 비고 `*` = policy-designated priority student.

### Strategic implication
For capped-at-12 courses (ECO/UIC First type), bidding is **not** a lever — you bid 12,
everyone bids 12, and placement is decided by the tie-break ladder:
1. higher mileage (irrelevant when all bid the cap)
2. special-education status
3. **designated major of offering dept**
4. more courses applied (≤6 counted)
5. graduation applicants
6. first-time takers (not repeating)
7. credits-earned ratio

For 36-cap courses (UIC CC like UIC1251), bid size *is* the lever, and avg 19.1 means
a ~20M bid is competitive, ~36M is safe.

**Revised mileage model:** budget pressure is lower than feared — Micro+Macro cost 12M
each (not more), and they cannot be over-bid. Plan A's Sem 6 pinch needs re-checking
against actual per-course caps rather than assumed 12M×4 = 48M.
